STUCK AT ANY POINT? ASK THE APP ITSELF
   Click the gold "🎬 Producer" button at the bottom-right of ANY tab and ask your
   question in plain words — "how do I put myself in a scene?", "where is the clip
   tool?", "why can't I find my video?". It knows every tab and button of this studio
   and answers with the exact steps. Use its 📖 Detailed / ⚡ Brief buttons to choose
   between full instructions and a quick summary.

FIRST TIME YOU RUN IT
   1. Double-click NIHILPOINTZERO-OS-portable.exe — ONCE. (Or use the "NIHILPOINTZERO-OS"
      shortcut on your Desktop — it points straight at this exe.)
   2. The first launch takes ~40-60 seconds with no window showing (it's unpacking).
      WAIT. Do not double-click again.
   3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": More info -> Run anyway (it's safe,
      just unsigned).
   4. Nothing else is required. The AI brain is free and already chosen for you; you do
      not need to buy anything, ever, and you do not need to open Settings to start.
   5. The ONE thing worth doing once: Settings -> "Connect YouTube" -> "Show me how".
      Free, about three minutes, and Google never asks for a card. Without it the "Your
      channel" tab, the questions from your comments and the competitor gaps have
      nothing to read. It walks you through it with a button for each page and then
      tests the key for real, so you find out straight away if something is wrong
      instead of a week later.

HOW TO KNOW YOU'RE ON THE NEWEST BUILD  (READ THIS — it caused a lot of confusion)
   • The sidebar shows a small gold badge under "OS", e.g.
        v0.1.1 · 2026-07-19 15:59 · 4e5e704
     That reads: version · the exact date+time the build was made · a code id.
     The badge is now written AUTOMATICALLY at build time (it can never be forgotten),
     and it IS your proof of which build is running. Older/missing badge = stale copy.
   • PORTABLE EXE ONLY: do not pin it to the taskbar/Start while it's open — Windows
     freezes that pin to a TEMPORARY unpacked copy and keeps launching OLD code forever.
     Launch it from the Desktop shortcut or the .exe in this folder.
   • INSTALLED VERSION (run NIHILPOINTZERO-OS-setup.exe once): pinning is completely
     SAFE — it runs from a fixed location, so pins always launch the current code, and
     it opens in seconds. This is the recommended way to use the studio on your own PC;
     keep the portable exe for USB travel.

IF THE AI STOPS ANSWERING  (READ THIS — it was a real bug, now fixed)
   The free online AI this app shipped with STOPPED BEING FREE (their decision, not a
   fault on your PC). It now replies "payment required" or "model not found".
   • The app now falls back automatically to Ollama — the free AI that runs on YOUR PC.
     If answers still come, that's where they're coming from. Nothing to do.
   • Every AI failure is now written down. Settings -> "Known Issues" shows exactly what
     went wrong and when, with the service's own words. The full file is at
     nihilpointzero-data\logs\ai-errors.log
   • Settings -> "🩺 Run full check" now really tests the AI (it used to check the wrong
     address, which is why it stayed green during the whole outage).
   • For the best free setup: keep Ollama installed and running. No key, no internet
     needed, nothing to pay.

REAL AI VIDEO — WHAT'S NEW, AND WHAT'S STILL TRUE  (being straight with you)
   NEW: Video Studio now has "REAL AI video — free cloud" — real moving video per
   scene, not a photo slideshow. TWO free routes (pick in Settings -> AI Video):
   • Pollinations (recommended here): sign up at enter.pollinations.ai with GitHub
     or email — NO phone number — create a free key and paste the SECRET (sk_…) one
     into Settings. Free "Pollen" comes from that dashboard's QUESTS tab: claim the
     rewards you already qualify for (e.g. "Create your first API key" pays 0.25 —
     about five 5-second scenes). A "Test key" button in Settings shows your balance
     without spending anything.
   • Puter (Google's Veo): no key at all — a sign-in window pops up during the first
     build. Heads-up: Puter's verification rejects some countries' phone numbers
     (Pakistani numbers included) — if their sign-up won't finish, use Pollinations.
   The honest catches for both: the free allowances are small; only up to 5 scenes
   per build get real motion (the rest use AI stills — adjustable in Settings); and
   each real-motion scene takes minutes. When an allowance runs out, scenes fall
   back to AI still images and the build log says why. Nothing breaks.
   STILL TRUE: LOCAL AI "motion video" and "talking photo" models (LTX-Video, Wan 2.2,
   SadTalker, LivePortrait) need a DEDICATED NVIDIA graphics card. This laptop's
   graphics are built into the processor, so those cannot run here at all — not even
   slowly. The option is now visible but greyed out ("Requires NVIDIA GPU — not
   detected on this system"), and you can set it all up in Settings -> AI Video
   today — it unlocks by itself the day this PC has an NVIDIA card.
   Also free: photo slideshows from AI images (16 styles), real stock footage, your
   own footage and recordings, narrated in natural voices.

RECORDING SO IT DOESN'T LOOK LIKE A PHONE
   Recorder tab -> pick Camera, Screen, or "Voice only - no face".
   • The number that matters is the bitrate, and the Recorder now sets it to exactly
     what YouTube asks uploads to be (8 Mbps at 1080p30, 45 at 4K30). Browsers
     otherwise default to about 2.5 Mbps at ANY size, which is why phone recordings
     usually look soft. The Recorder shows you the figure and the size per minute.
   • Choose the frame rate too: 24 (cinematic), 30 (normal), 60 (smooth, best for
     screens and movement).
   • "Voice only" opens no camera at all - no permission prompt, no camera light. Your
     narration is saved as an audio track to put under scenes or stock footage.
   • Filming on the phone: in the phone app, "Film it now" opens your phone's OWN camera
     app rather than recording inside the page. That uses the real sensor, hardware
     encoding, stabilisation and autofocus, and is by a distance the best-looking
     option. Front camera for your face, back camera for everything else.
   • Long takes recorded on the phone are saved to the phone's Downloads rather than
     squeezed over the link. Short ones go straight to the PC.

WHAT IS NEW (1 August 2026)
   You never have to remember this list. Open Settings and read "What changed" — it
   shows what is new in the build you are actually running, and only things that are
   really in it. Nothing is ever listed before the code for it has shipped.

   The short version of this round:

   CONNECTING YOUTUBE IS NOW A WALKTHROUGH, NOT AN INSTRUCTION. Settings used to say
   "get a free key from Google Cloud Console, enable YouTube Data API v3, create an API
   key" and leave you there. Now it is five numbered steps with a button on each that
   opens the exact page. You paste the key, press Check, and the app really tries it —
   if something is wrong it tells you in plain English which step to go back to. The
   most common problem, by a mile, is a perfectly good key on a project where the
   YouTube service was never switched on, and the app now says exactly that rather than
   letting you assume you typed the key wrong.

   AND IT FINDS YOUR CHANNEL FOR YOU. Type your @name — the one on your own channel
   page — and it looks up the long "UC..." ID and shows you the channel name it found,
   so you can see it picked the right one. YouTube buries that ID three menus deep and
   nobody knows it by heart.

   WHEN A CHANNEL PANEL COMES BACK EMPTY IT NOW SAYS WHY. It used to print "no videos
   could be read, check your key and channel ID in Settings" no matter what had
   happened, and only one of the seven things that can actually happen was that. In
   particular, "could not reach YouTube" is now shown in amber and says plainly that it
   does not know — it is not the app telling you that you have no videos. Amber always
   means "I could not tell", and is never something for you to go and fix.

   A NEW TAB, "Your Channel". Everything else in the app helps you make a video. This
   one tells you what has already worked. It reads your own past videos and works out
   which title shapes did better FOR YOU — not what advice says works in general, which
   is an average across millions of channels that are not yours. It also tells you which
   day and hour your videos really get watched, and it always shows how many videos each
   claim is based on. If there is not enough history to be honest, it says so instead of
   guessing.

   THE SAME TAB READS YOUR COMMENTS. It finds the questions, groups the ones asking the
   same thing (in English and Roman Urdu together), and ranks them by how many different
   people asked. Every question is quoted word for word from a real comment, so you can
   check it yourself. The same question asked forty times is a video with an audience
   before you record a frame.

   IT ALSO FINDS YOUR SERIES. If your titles say "#4" or "Part 2" or "Hissa 2", it works
   out which videos belong together, warns you if a number is missing or used twice, and
   writes the description links, the pinned comment and the end-screen line for you.

   HEAR YOUR SCRIPT BEFORE YOU RECORD IT. Script Writer -> "Read it to me". A script is
   spoken, not read, and reading it silently hides the things that cost you a retake: a
   sentence too long to say in one breath, a number you cannot read off the page, a word
   written twice in a row that your eye skips over. It reads it to you at double speed —
   a twelve-minute script takes six minutes to check — and lists what to listen for with
   the time each one happens.

   FIVE DIFFERENT OPENINGS. Script Writer -> "Show 5 openings". The first fifteen seconds
   decide whether the rest gets watched, and they are the hardest part to judge from
   inside your own draft. It shows the same material five ways, using only sentences
   already in your script, and shows which sentence each one came from.

   YOUR NUMBERS, CHECKED. Put the figures you looked up in the "Verified data" box, one
   per line, like "Reserves, 2026-07-31: 11.2". The app now reads that back against your
   script and flags any figure it cannot trace. One wrong number is the comment that gets
   pinned, and a published video cannot be edited. It also writes the sources block for
   your description.

   CUT THE DEAD AIR. Video Studio -> any video -> "What would be cut?". It tells you
   first, then removes the long pauses when you say "Cut it", keeping a quarter-second of
   breath so it still sounds like a person talking. It makes a NEW video. Your original
   is never touched.

   CHARTS THAT DRAW THEMSELVES. Charts -> "Draw it on". Record the screen while it plays
   and lay it over your narration.

   AND SIX THINGS YOU WILL NOT SEE, ONLY NOTICE. Every render now: builds in one pass
   instead of four, so less picture quality is thrown away; delivers audio at the level
   YouTube itself wants, so it stops quietly turning your videos down; puts slow camera
   movement on footage instead of leaving a frozen frame; makes scenes tighten toward the
   end, where videos lose people; lands b-roll on the word it belongs to; and runs a
   one-second check first, so a problem stops you before the render does, not twenty
   minutes in.

ONE VERSION, EVERYWHERE — AND HOW TO CHECK
   There is only ever ONE live version. When something is upgraded, the old copy is
   replaced everywhere, not left running next to it. That means all five of these
   always match: this laptop's studio folder, the installed app, GitHub, and BOTH
   the phone routes.

   HOW TO CHECK, in five seconds each:
   • On the laptop — the small gold badge in the sidebar under "OS". It reads
     version, date-time and a short code id (like `a1b2c3d`). That code id is the
     only honest proof of which build you are running.
   • On the phone app — Settings, right at the bottom: "This app's version". Same
     idea. There is also a "Check for a newer version" button.

   IF THE PHONE SEEMS OLD: it caches itself on the handset so it can work with no
   signal, which is normally what you want. It now notices a new version and reloads
   itself. If it ever does not, open Settings and press "Check for a newer version",
   or close the tab and reopen it.

   IF THE LAPTOP SEEMS OLD: the installer alone never upgrades anything — it just
   installs whichever file is sitting there. A new file only appears after a build.
   See "If updates don't show up" in MEGA-DIAGNOSTIC-REPORT.md.

AM I UP TO DATE? (one look, any time)
   Settings -> the Version box at the top. It says plainly whether you are up to date,
   and shows which version you are running next to the newest published one. There is a
   "Check now" button if you want it re-checked this second.
   If it says "could not check just now", that means exactly that - it does NOT mean you
   are up to date. It will never claim a result it did not get.

YOU DO NOT HAVE TO UPDATE IT AT ALL (this is the normal case now)
   The studio opens by itself when you turn the laptop on. At that moment nobody is
   waiting for it, so that is when it quietly installs anything new. By the time you
   sit down it is already the newest version and there is nothing to press.

   Two things it will never do: it will not update while a render or a queue is
   running, and it will not interrupt you if YOU opened the app yourself — then you
   get the blue notice and you choose when.

   You can turn this off in Settings ("Open the studio when Windows starts"). If you
   do, updating goes back to the one button described next.

HOW TO UPDATE BY HAND — LET THE APP DO IT
   Open the app. If a newer version exists, a blue notice appears at the bottom of
   the window saying "A newer version of the app exists". Press "Get the update".
   That is everything. The app downloads the update itself, checks it is the right
   file, starts it, and closes so it can finish. Windows will ask you once whether
   to allow it — say yes. Then the app reopens, updated.

   You do not open a web page. You do not go looking in Downloads. You do not need
   File Explorer. If the download is interrupted, press the button again — it keeps
   what it already got.

HOW TO UPDATE THE WHOLE WORKSHOP TOO (only if you have the code folder)
   Open the NihilPointZeroStudio-workshop folder on your PC and double-click
   UPDATE-MY-STUDIO.cmd. This does more than the button above: as well as the app,
   it refreshes the code folder and your Desktop studio folder and pushes to GitHub.
   Five to ten minutes, and you can walk away while it runs.

   It checks its tools first and tells you by name if something is missing. If any
   step fails it STOPS and says so in plain words rather than leaving things
   half-updated, and it never touches your nihilpointzero-data folder — your videos,
   scripts and settings are not at risk either way.

   When it finishes it tells you to double-click NIHILPOINTZERO-OS-setup.exe once.
   Do that; it keeps all your work.

   NO CODE FOLDER ON THIS PC? Then just download the finished app:
     1. go to github.com/DSKJazz/NihilPointZeroStudio/releases/latest
     2. under "Assets", click NIHILPOINTZERO-OS-setup.exe
     3. open your Downloads folder and double-click it
   That page is rebuilt automatically whenever the code changes, so whatever is on
   it is always the newest — you never have to check.
